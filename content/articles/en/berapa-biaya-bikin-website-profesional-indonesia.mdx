## Why Website Prices Can Differ by Up to 100x

Google "website cost" and you'll find Rp500,000 in one place and Rp50 million in another — for something both call a "website." This isn't a scam. It's two entirely different camps of providers selling genuinely different products under the same label.

## The Two Camps in Indonesia's Website Market

**Camp 1 — Mass-market/template vendors.** Company-profile websites priced around **Rp500,000 – Rp1.5 million**. These are usually ready-made templates where only the logo and text get swapped in. Fast and cheap, but the design is generic and often reused by other businesses — you won't be the only one with that exact look.

**Camp 2 — Established agencies.** For a company-profile site with custom design, prices run **Rp5 million – Rp80 million**, depending on complexity. What you're paying for here isn't just "a finished page" — it's strategy, design built specifically around your brand, and a proven track record with real clients.

The problem is, that second camp often feels like too big a leap for a business just trying to graduate from a free template — paying tens of millions for something that isn't guaranteed to pay for itself right away.

## Why Cheap Isn't Always Actually Cheaper

A Rp500,000 website feels like a bargain at first, but the real cost usually shows up later: the same template used by a competitor, no structure for SEO, no one to call when something breaks technically, and usually a full rebuild from scratch the moment your business needs more (like a booking system or a dynamic product catalog).

That said, more expensive doesn't automatically mean better either. What matters isn't the number upfront, but **what's actually included**: is the copywriting tailored to your brand, is there animation/interaction that makes it feel alive, is baseline SEO in place, and is there support after the site goes live.

## How to Evaluate Any Website Offer, Whoever the Vendor

Before you commit to anyone (TRIGIA included), ask these 4 questions:

1. **How many revisions are included?** If it's not stated, it usually means zero — and extra revisions may cost more
2. **Is the design custom or template-based?** Ask to see real past work, not sample mockups
3. **Is there post-launch support?** A site that breaks two weeks after launch with no one to contact is a real problem, not a rare edge case
4. **Is domain & hosting included or separate?** This yearly cost is often "hidden" and only surfaces after you've already committed
